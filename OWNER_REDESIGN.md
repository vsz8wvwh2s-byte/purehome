# PureHome redesign

PureHome is an owner-operated cleaning service, not a provider marketplace.

Offer regular and deep cleaning for houses, apartments, and small offices. Customers may request one-time, weekly, biweekly, or monthly cleaning without creating an account. They provide contact information, property details, preferred time, and optional photos. The owner provides supplies, reviews each request, and sends a personalized estimate. No payment occurs on request. All bookings require owner approval.
